## PURPOSE

This matrix controls completion from the learner's point of view.

The app is not complete merely because it contains many questions.
It is complete only when a prospective learner can work through the official study areas repeatedly offline and obtain broad, source-verified coverage of what they are expected to know.

The official learner-preparation core is:
1. Rules of the Road
2. Road Traffic Signs, Signals and Markings
3. Vehicle Controls / Components

The app also includes:
4. Learner-process readiness
5. Digital CLLT familiarity
6. Practical road-use application

## COVERAGE STATUS KEY

- COVERED — represented in current bank
- DEEPEN — represented, but more variations are required
- TO BUILD — material topic not yet adequately represented
- VEHICLE-SPECIFIC — audit separately for Code 1 / Code 2
- ORIENTATION — learner-readiness content, not necessarily scored
- SOURCE GAP — verify before writing questions

# E. DIGITAL CLLT READINESS — ORIENTATION

Verified preparation:
- touchscreen / computer environment;
- one-question-at-a-time interaction practice;
- selecting an answer;
- visually recognising selected/unselected states;
- concentration across randomised questions;
- answer-option randomisation awareness;
- completing a mock digitally;
- offline repetition before test day.

Do NOT claim unverified official behaviour for:
- exact duration;
- Back / Next behaviour;
- skipping;
- review screen;
- answer changing;
- timer display;
- exact section order.

# F. PRACTICAL APPLICATION LAYER

Every important rule/sign/control family should eventually include at least one question that asks the learner what to DO in a simple road situation, not only what a definition means.

Examples of desired application:
- approaching a pedestrian crossing;
- reacting to an emergency vehicle;
- merging traffic;
- poor-weather following distance;
- railway crossing;
- broken traffic signals;
- stopping / parking choices;
- overtaking visibility;
- turning across oncoming traffic;
- interpreting markings and lane arrows.

This layer builds confidence and transfers knowledge into road-safety judgement without pretending to replace the later K53 practical driving test.

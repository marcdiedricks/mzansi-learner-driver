## PURPOSE

The app is complete only when a prospective Code 1 or Code 2 learner can repeatedly practise the material study scope offline, not merely when a numerical question target is reached.

Completion therefore uses two tests:

1. **depth** — enough questions for repeated randomised practice; and  
2. **breadth** — every material learner-study subject family is represented.

The app does not reproduce confidential CLLT questions and is not labelled an exact official CLLT simulator.

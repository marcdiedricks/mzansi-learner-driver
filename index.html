LEARN

Build your knowledge

Work through the three core areas at your own pace.

01

Rules of the Road

Learn the rules for safe and lawful road use, including intersections, overtaking, stopping, parking, freeways, pedestrians, towing, tyres and accident duties.

02

Road Signs, Signals & Markings

Recognise regulatory and warning signs, traffic signals, road markings and guidance information.

03

Vehicle Controls & Components

Understand the controls and major components for your selected Code 1 or Code 2 pathway.

04

Road-Use Application

Practise deciding what the safest lawful response is in ordinary road situations without replacing supervised practical training.

05

Digital Test Confidence

Get comfortable selecting answers, changing a choice and moving through a computer-style practice session.

06

Offline Repetition

Use the full bank repeatedly offline, revisit weak areas and build confidence without using mobile data every time.

## Working rules

1. English remains the source-master meaning.
2. Afrikaans should use the same term consistently across question, options and explanation.
3. isiXhosa must not randomly alternate between borrowed technical terms and formal equivalents.
4. Borrowed technical terms already understood by learners may remain provisionally where they are clearer, but only a competent isiXhosa reviewer may approve that choice.
5. Any terminology correction must be applied consistently across all 10 Code 1-specific questions.
6. Functional phone rendering does not equal language approval.
7. Until human review is complete, AF/XH statuses remain LANGUAGE_QA_PENDING.

## Focused human-review list

The reviewer only needs to decide these items:

- whether `ibrake / ilever / ithrottle / iswitshi` are the clearest learner terms in isiXhosa;
- whether `ilever yeklatshi` and `ilever yegiya` should remain as familiar technical borrowings;
- whether `umkhweli wesithuthuthu` or another consistent term should be used for motorcycle rider;
- whether the current helmet/passenger/gearbox wording is natural and easily understood.

Everything else should remain unchanged unless the reviewer identifies a real clarity or meaning problem.

## Release rule

Do not mark any Code 1 AF/XH item LANGUAGE_QA_APPROVED until the terminology choices above are confirmed by a competent human reviewer.
